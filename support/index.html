What to include in a request

  • Your Stemy version: choose Stemy › About Stemy, or open Stemy › Settings › General › About.
  • Your macOS version: choose Apple menu › About This Mac.
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that make the problem happen again, if you know them.
  • If the problem involves a particular song, its file format (for example WAV, AIFF or MP3). Please don't attach audio files unless we ask for them.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.
